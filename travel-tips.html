Quick Cultural Tips

heading-text-alignment: left faq-text-alignment: left faq-loop: - question: Do I need cash for border crossings and road trips? answer-html: Yes! Even in big cities, cash is king for things like fuel, tolls, and especially border fees. We’ve been caught out more than once assuming cards would work everywhere – don’t make our mistake. Make sure to take small bills or coiins too, for small tips to the services workers as a nice gesture. - question: Should I download offline maps? answer-html: 100% if you're ventuing into rural areas. Phone signal can disappear fast in the desert or on rural roads. Download Google Maps (whom we lovingly call Nancy) offline for your whole route, and save your hotel/location pins before you lose signal. If you're in the bigger cities, connection shouldn't be an issue. - question: Does it really get cold in the desert? answer-html: Shockingly, yes – especially in winter! Desert places like AlUla get cold at night; I mean like below freezing cold. Pack warm layers, even if it’s 30°C(86°F) during the day. Desert sands don't keep the heat from the daytime like other types of land do, so when the sun dips below the horizon, or even if you're sitting in the shade, the heat disappears quickly. - question: Can I rely on Taxis, Uber or Careem everywhere? answer-html: Not at all. In smaller towns and even parts of AlUla, ride-hailing is non-existent or very slow. Renting a car is usually your best bet. However, in cities like Cairo, Uber is your best bet for getting fair prices for rides, while in Dubai, the cities taxis are easy to catch and always run the meter fairly. - question: What if I get stuck or need help? answer-html: Don’t be shy! Especially in Saudi, locals are usually eager to help and proud to show off their hospitality.
