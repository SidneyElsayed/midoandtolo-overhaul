What Makes Saudi Overlanding Unique?

description_html: Forget what you know about road trips elsewhere. In Saudi, you’ll find endless empty highways, actual wild camping, and a landscape that changes from red dunes to volcanic rock to palm-filled oases. Gas is cheap, distances are huge, and you’ll meet more camels than cars outside the cities. button-1-text: button-1-link: button-2-text: button-2-link: image: /images/midoandtolo-driving-in-raptor.jpg image-placement: right image-horizontal-focal-point: center image-vertical-focal-point: center text-placement: center large-text-alignment: center scheme-name: dark - id: text-banner heading_html:

Before You Hit the Road – Essentials Checklist

Pro Tips from the Saudi Road

heading-text-alignment: left faq-text-alignment: left faq-loop: - question: Is wild camping really allowed everywhere? answer-html: Yes, in most areas! You can pitch a tent almost anywhere outside urban zones or protected parks. Just be respectful – leave no trace and avoid private farms or obvious “no entry” signs. - question: How do you find the best camp spots? answer-html: We scout on Google Earth, ask locals, or just drive around until we find a spot that calls our name for the night. The best spots are often just out of sight from the main road, with a view of something epic. - question: What’s the biggest rookie mistake? answer-html: Underestimating distance and fuel needs. We’ve run out of fuel mid-journey before - on our way to the Medinah airport no less! Luckily the locals are very helpful, and someone quickly came to our rescue. - question: What are the real dangers? answer-html: Camels on the road at night (seriously, they’re invisible), sudden sandstorms, and sometimes getting stuck in soft sand. In the hotter months, critters like snakes and scorpions are a potential risk as well. Always tell someone your route if you’re going remote. - question: How do Saudis react to campers? answer-html: Usually with curiosity and kindness! We’ve had locals invite us for tea, bring us food, and even help dig us out when stuck. scheme-name: featured - id: text-banner heading_html:
