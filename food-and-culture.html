Quick Cultural Tips

heading-text-alignment: left faq-text-alignment: left faq-loop: - question: How should I greet people? answer-html: Always greet with a smile and a warm “Salaam!” which means peace. It’s an easy enough word to learna dn the best way to start any conversation. Locals truly appreciate the effort. - question: What should I wear? answer-html: When in doubt, dress modestly, especially in traditional areas. Covering shoulders and knees is the norm, and it shows respect for local customs. For women, carrying a scarf to cover your head is a good idea, in case you want to enter any mosques or holy sites. Now this isn't necessarily the case everywhere - nightlife in Cairo, walking the marina in Dubai, or heading to the beach in Dahab - there's plenty of diversity in what people wear across the region and for what occasions. - question: Is it rude to turn down tea or coffee? answer-html: Accepting tea or coffee is a sign of friendship. Even if you’re not thirsty, sharing a cup is about hospitality, so take time to enjoy the moment - even just a sip if it's really not your thing. It's also not that serious if you say no, so don't feel like you'll make a cultural faux pas by declining. - question: Anything special about Fridays? answer-html: Fridays are the main day of rest and prayer. Expect slower mornings and many shops to open later.
